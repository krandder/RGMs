# The generator's depth and the best depth for prediction need not coincide

After 512 training transitions, depth four had the lowest expected log loss among the four fixed-depth predictors in five of these six worlds. In two worlds generated by a depth-two tree, a depth-four predictor did better than the depth-two predictor. In the third, depth three scored best. These are comparisons of fitted forecasts under the specified model and prior. They do not establish that the world needs four levels.

| Generator depth | Seed | Depth 1 | Depth 2 | Depth 3 | Depth 4 | All-depth mixture |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 0 | .173 | .140 | .102 | **.090** | .090 |
| 2 | 1 | .139 | .094 | .076 | **.060** | .060 |
| 2 | 2 | .142 | .088 | **.074** | .076 | .076 |
| 4 | 0 | .127 | .089 | .050 | **.036** | .036 |
| 4 | 1 | .144 | .100 | .055 | **.047** | .047 |
| 4 | 2 | .163 | .121 | .096 | **.064** | .090 |

Entries are expected excess log loss in nats per transition (lower is better). Each depth's predictor averages over all trees at that depth with their posterior weights. The mixture averages across depths with their posterior probabilities. To score the fitted predictors, we used the generator's known transition matrix and propagated the initial leaf distribution over 512 steps, avoiding held-out outcome sampling noise. Training never uses that matrix. The shallowest model is a flat Markov matrix: all 16 leaf states remain distinct at depth one. It has the most free transition parameters: 240, compared with 128, 68 and 44 at depths two, three and four. Its finite-data score says nothing about an inability to represent the dynamics with enough observations.

This analysis was added after the original six outcomes were known. It uses their exact same training histories. Mixing the conditional forecasts reproduces each previously stored predictive matrix; on four and eight leaves, direct enumeration agrees with each conditional forecast. [Raw scores](predictive_depth.json) also include the realized held-out loss relative to the generator. Reproduce from the fork root:

```sh
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 nice -n 19 python3 -m experiments.hierarchy.subset_dp.predictive_depth --out /tmp/predictive-depth.json
```
